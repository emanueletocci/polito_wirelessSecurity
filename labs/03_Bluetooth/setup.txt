--- Setup ---
Alice's PC: CachyOS (Kernel: 7.0.8-1-cachyos-bore)
Alice's device: Bose QuietComfort 45 (QC-45) Bluetooth headphones
Trudy's PC: ParrotOS 7.2 (Live USB, Kernel: 6.19.13-parrot7-amd64)

Timeline:
Alice's Device connects to Alice's PC different times, while Trudy passively sniffs the BLE traffic.
During the capture, Alice's device rotates its RPA, which are tracked by Trudy using a static payload fingerprint to demonstrate linkability despite the privacy features. 
Specifically, 7 distinct address rotations (7 different RPAs) were successfully identified during the sniffing session.

--- Useful Filters ---

On Alice's PC we used the following filter to find advertisement packets coming from Alice's device RPA addresses
- (bthci_evt.bd_addr == ac:bf:71:81:be:22) && (bthci_evt.le_meta_subevent == 0x0d) && (bthci_evt.le_peer_address_type != 0x01)

On Trudy's PC we used the following filtering payloads to obtain RPA addresses 
- (btcommon.eir_ad.entry.device_name == "LE-Thunder") || (btcommon.eir_ad.entry.data == 51:10:ac:83:6e:59:e5:46:0b:59:13)
